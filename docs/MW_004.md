# Part A — Role-Based Login & Demo Account

## A1. One login page for everyone

Companies and customers log in from the **same `/login` page** (email or phone + password). There is no separate company login. After a successful login, the app routes by the account's **role** and, for companies, the **application status**:

| Account                                                       | Redirect to                                                               |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Customer (verified)                                           | `/app/new-request`                                                        |
| Company — status `approved`                                   | `/company/overview`                                                       |
| Company — `pending_review` / `changes_requested` / `rejected` | `/company/application-status` (status page from the company sign-up task) |
| Unverified email (any role)                                   | `/verify-email`                                                           |

**Route guards:**

- `/app/*` → customers only.
- `/company/*` → companies only.
- A user opening the other portal's URL is redirected to their own portal, never shown an error page.
- The login response (or `GET /api/me`) returns `role` and `companyStatus`. The frontend must not guess the role from the email.
- Logout is available in both portals and clears the session.

## A2. Demo company account

Create **one demo company account** so the team can log in and use the company portal.

- Created by a **seed script** (not by hand in the dashboard).
- Role `company`, email verified, application status **approved**, with a complete company profile.
- Use the demo company from the HTML as its data: **"أليكس إكسبريس للشحن الدولي واللوجستيات"**, alias "الناقل الذهبي", activity: international shipping & customs.
- Email: `demo.company@mirhalgo.com` (or any agreed address).
- **Password comes from an environment variable** (`DEMO_COMPANY_PASSWORD`). Never commit it to the repo. Share it with the team privately.
- Seed it in **development and staging only**. Production gets it only if the Team Lead approves.
- All mock portal data (Part B) belongs to this account.

# Part B — Company Portal (Frontend Only)

## B1. Mock data layer

- Same approach as the customer portal: **TanStack Query + MSW**, realistic fixtures, ~400 ms latency.
- **Convert the demo's data into JSON fixtures:**
  - The companies list (≈6), so competitor offers have something to compare against.
  - The orders (≈28).
  - All price offers, including competitors' offers.
  - The client inquiries.
  - Rewrite any colloquial Arabic in the fixtures in formal Arabic.
- **Mocks must behave like the future backend**:
  - Order responses for companies **never include client contact fields** (name, phone, WhatsApp). Filter them out in the mock handler, not in the UI.
  - Competitor offers are returned **anonymized**: no company ID or name.
  - Offers from companies with "hide my offers" enabled are **not returned at all**. Only their count is returned.
- Mutations (submit/edit offer, reply to inquiry, settings) update the mock state and **persist in `localStorage` per account**, so the demo survives a refresh.
- Settings → **"Reset demo data"** button, shown in development/staging only.

## B5. Submit / edit offer modal

The form adapts automatically to the order's category. **All template fields are optional; the total cost is required.**

| Template           | Used for              | Fields                                                                                    | Total cost        |
| ------------------ | --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Sea & customs      | Sea freight & customs | Ocean freight · THC · B/L fee · Telex release · Transit time · Free time · Departure date | **Dual currency** |
| International land | International land    | Vehicle type · Transport cost · Transit time · Loading date · Border crossing · Notes     | **Dual currency** |
| Domestic land      | Domestic land         | Vehicle type · Transport cost · Transit time · Loading date · Notes                       | EGP only          |
| Air                | Air                   | Air freight cost · Airline · Flight date · Transit time · Extra charges                   | **Dual currency** |
| China              | SHEIN & Chinese sites | Weight cost · Product purchase cost · Shipping duration                                   | EGP only          |

**Total cost rules**

- **Dual currency:** a foreign amount (USD or EUR) **and/or** an EGP amount. At least one is required. Both can be entered together.
- **EGP only:** a single amount, required.
- **Amount format:** positive numbers only; Arabic digits are converted to Western digits.
- **Display:** money is shown as "1,000 دولار + 38,000 جنيه" or "5,200 جنيه".

**Business rules**

- A company **can submit more than one offer** on the same order.
- An offer can be **edited only while "Under review"**. Edit mode pre-fills all values, and the offer stays "Under review" after saving.
- If the admin has **suspended offer submission** for the company, submitting is blocked. Show a toast and a disabled state with an explanation.
- **Offer IDs** must be unique. The demo's count-based IDs can collide, so don't reuse that logic.
- Use the **real current date** (the demo hard-codes one).
- **After submit or edit:**
  - Show a success toast.
  - Add an entry to the activity log.
  - Update all affected lists and badges, plus the KPIs.

## B6. Our submitted offers (`/company/offers`)

- Note: "Offers can only be edited while under review. You can submit more than one offer on the same request."
- **Stats bar:** total · under review · accepted · rejected.
- Filter by status (all / under review / accepted / rejected) and search by offer ID or order ID.
- **Grouped by order:**
  - Groups are sorted by their latest offer. Each group header shows order ID, service, number of our offers and an **Order details** link.
  - Each offer card shows ID, "new" badge, status badge, date, template name and total.
  - A rejected offer also shows its **rejection reason**.
  - Each card's colored stripe follows its status.
- **Card actions:**
  - **Edit offer**, only while under review.
  - **Compare prices**, only when the order has more than one offer.
- Opening a card removes its "new" badge.

## B7. Competitor offers (`/company/competitors`)

- **Intro note:** competitors' names are hidden, and companies that chose to hide their offers are not shown.
- **Which orders appear:** orders where we are assigned or have offered **and** at least one other company has offered.
- **Table columns:**
  - Order ID.
  - Service.
  - **Our offer(s):** totals, or "we have not submitted an offer yet".
  - Number of visible competitor offers.
  - **Compare** button.
- **Comparison modal:**
  - Our offers (ID, status, template, date, total).
  - Each visible competitor as **"Competitor company #N"** (status, template, date, total).
  - A line such as "+ 2 companies chose to hide their offers".
  - Never show a competitor's name, ID or alias.

## B8. Client inquiries (`/company/inquiries`)

- **Intro note:** every inquiry is linked automatically to its order and to our offer on it.
- **Filter and search:** filter by status (all / awaiting reply / replied); search by order ID or inquiry ID. Newest first.
- **Inquiry card:** ID, "new" badge, status, order ID with category icon, date, question preview and an open button.
- **Inquiry modal**:
  - **Order summary:** order ID, service, first 3 visible fields, link to the full order details. No client contact data.
  - **Our offer(s) on this order:** ID, status, total.
  - **The client's question.**
  - **Reply** textarea (required) → "Send reply". The reply reaches the client through the platform administration. Status becomes "Replied".
  - **Change from the demo:** after a reply is sent, show it **read-only** with its date. The demo lets the company overwrite it.

## B10. Shared UI components

- **Toasts:** success / error / info.
- **Confirmation modal:** for any destructive or important action.
- **Buttons:** loading state that blocks double clicks.
- **Modals:** lock page scroll while open; close with Esc or a click outside; focus trap.
- **List states:** loading skeletons, empty states and error states in every list.
- **Text rendering:** never render user content as raw HTML. No `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The demo company logs in from the normal `/login` page and lands on `/company/overview`. A customer lands on `/app`.
- [ ] Customers cannot open `/company/*` and companies cannot open `/app/*`. Non-approved companies see the application status page.
- [ ] The demo account is created by a seed script, and its password comes from an environment variable (not in the repo).
- [ ] All 6 sections and all modals from `Company_vieww.html` work with mock data: badges, "new" markers, search, filters, grouping, comparison, offer templates, inquiry replies and settings.
- [ ] Offer rules enforced: total required, dual-currency logic, edit only while under review, multiple offers allowed, blocked when submission is suspended.
- [ ] Privacy enforced in the mock API: no client contact data, anonymized competitors, hidden-offer companies excluded.
- [ ] Mock changes persist across refresh; "Reset demo data" works in dev/staging.
- [ ] Logout available; responsive; AR/EN with RTL/LTR; formal Arabic only.
